If you want to participate in this tradition, we recommend visiting the gold shops in Istanbul. Hopefully seeking out a red-ribbon adorned gold coin will be part of the novelty of the travel. There will be a time during the ceremony in olden times guests would have pinned the coins to the bride and groom's clothing, but in a nod to modernity we will have a basket for the coins.

Funds raised will go towards buying the mom-to-be an electric bike, followed by an electric bike for the dad-to-be to be able to keep up with the aforementioned mom-to-be. If we break the two-bike dollar amount, funds will go towards a dutch canal boat, which you should all come visit us for a ride.
